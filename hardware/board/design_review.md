# Controller board: design-review checklist (v1 schematic-as-code)

Status: schematic only, generated by `board/schematic.py`. No layout, nothing fabricated or measured.
Automated today (`pytest tests/test_board.py`): own ERC (required pins connected, no shorted rails,
decoupling on every IC supply net, inputs driven, MCU pin map == netlist), SKiDL ERC, e-stop topology,
shunt Kelvin wiring, BOM completeness. Everything below needs a human with datasheets.

## A. Must check before layout
- [ ] **MCU pins / AFs** (board/out/mcu_pins.md): confirm every "verify" row in STM32CubeMX for
      STM32G474RETx: PB14 = USART3_DE (AF7), PB12/PB13 = FDCAN2_RX/TX (AF9), PC0/PC1 = ADC12_IN6/IN7,
      PA11/PA12 USB pin configuration. Confirm the recalled LQFP-64 pad numbers (or just swap in the
      KiCad symbol `MCU_ST_STM32G4:STM32G474RETx`; the netlist connects by pin name).
- [ ] Option bytes plan: nBOOT0 / nSWBOOT0 so PB8-BOOT0 behaves as intended (BOOT button -> USB DFU).
- [ ] **Servo bus logic level**: measure an STS3215's idle data-line level. The TX buffer (74AHCT1G126) drives
      5 V and the bus is pulled up to 5 V. If the servos are 3.3 V-only, swap U6 for 74LVC1G126 on +3V3 and
      move the pull-up to +3V3 (same SOT-23-5 footprint).
- [ ] Servo connector: housing, pitch and pin order of the real STS3215 lead (J5-J7 are placeholders). Each
      3-pin servo housing is typically rated ~3 A: the board offers 3 ports so no single connector carries the
      whole arm; route the chain as base+shoulder / elbow+wrist / roll+gripper.
- [ ] All parts marked `verify` / `TBD` in board/out/bom.csv (22 lines): CSD17573Q5B, AOD4185 (Vgs at
      -8 V, SOA for inrush), BSS138 at Vgs 3.3 V, TCAN1462V suffix/package, WSL2512 3 mOhm availability,
      crystal load capacitance (C = 2 x (CL - Cstray)), fuse, inductors, connectors.
- [ ] TPS54331 compensation (7.5k / 6.8n / 47p), 10 uH inductor and EN-divider UVLO (~7 V) recomputed with
      the datasheet design procedure / WEBENCH for 12 V -> 5 V at 1 A.
- [ ] LM74700 EN tied to ANODE (always on) is acceptable; check VCAP value (100 nF) and FET Qg.
- [ ] E-stop sense divider: 100k/33k gives ~2.7 V at 12 V (Vih 0.7*VDD = 2.31 V) and ~5.5 V at a 24 V
      surge on an FT pin (abs. max VDD+4 / 5.5 V). Acceptable, but consider 100k/30k + a 3.6 V clamp.
- [ ] Servo-rail inrush: P-FET turn-on is slowed by the 47 nF gate-drain cap with the 4.7k gate resistor
      (~ms ramp) into 2 x 470 uF; check FET SOA for that ramp.

## B. Safety logic (review the intent, then test on the first board)
- Servo power = P-FET Q2, gate pulled up to V12 (OFF) unless BOTH Q3 (driven by the e-stop loop) AND Q4
  (driven by MCU SERVO_PWR_EN, 100k pull-down = off in reset) conduct. Firmware can turn power off and can
  read the loop (ESTOP_SENSE) and the rail (VSERVO_ADC), but has no path that can turn power on while the
  loop is open. An unplugged e-stop, a broken wire or a loop shorted to GND all read as "stop".
- [ ] Bring-up test: with firmware forcing SERVO_PWR_EN high, open the loop -> V12_SERVO must drop
      (check fall time with servos stalled). Test with the MCU held in reset and with SWD halted.
- [ ] Decide whether the loop also needs a second, independent contact (two-channel e-stop) for v2.

## C. Layout constraints (to give the layout person)
- **High-current path** V12 input -> F1 -> Q1 -> Q2 -> R_SH -> servo ports, up to 16 A peak / ~9 A
  realistic: polygons on 2 layers, 2 oz outer copper if affordable, >= 6 mm wide where it is a trace, via
  arrays (>= 10 vias per layer change), bulk caps C_BULK right at the servo ports and at the input.
- **Shunt Kelvin**: INA240 IN+/IN- as a separate differential pair taken from the inner edges of the R_SH
  pads (4-terminal footprint style), routed together, no servo current in those traces; 100 nF on VS at the
  pin; INA240 output RC (100R/1n) at the MCU pin.
- **USB**: D+/D- 90 ohm differential, length-matched within 0.15 mm, no stubs; USBLC6 right at the
  connector; shield to GND through 1M || 4.7n.
- **Crystal**: Y1 and its 10 pF caps within ~5 mm of PF0/PF1, GND guard ring, no signals under it.
- **Bucks**: TPS54331 hot loop (VIN cap - high side switch - D2) as small as possible; SW node small; feedback
  taken at the output cap and routed away from L1. TLV62569 likewise.
- MCU decoupling: one 100 nF per VDD pin on the same side, VDDA/VREF+ caps after the ferrite.
- Servo data line: 100 ohm series resistor and TVS at the connectors; keep the buffer close to the ports.
- CAN: 120 ohm differential, split termination near J9, PESD2CAN at the connector.
- Thermal: Q2 (DPAK) and R_SH get copper area; NTC TH1 placed between Q2 and R_SH.
- Mounting: 4 x M3, connectors on one edge (power, servo, e-stop), USB/SWD on another.

## D. Not in v1 scope (noted)
- PCB layout, DRC, fab outputs; 4-layer stack-up choice; enclosure.
- Reverse-polarity protection of the servo ports themselves (miswired servo cable).
